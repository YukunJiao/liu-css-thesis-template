\swedishenglish{\chapter*{Ethics Statement}}{\chapter*{Ethics Statement}}

The study analyzes only publicly available, archival forum data with anonymized user identifiers and reports results exclusively at the aggregate level, avoiding any attempt at re-identification or evaluation of individual users.
Potential harm is minimized by paraphrasing any quoted material, limiting exposure to misogynistic or violent content, and focusing on patterns of interest.
The analysis treats all user groups consistently, avoids stigmatizing vulnerable individuals, and interprets findings in terms of structural and collective dynamics rather than attributing blame to specific users.
The study aims to advance public understanding of meaning-making and affective polarization without interfering with any specific community.
